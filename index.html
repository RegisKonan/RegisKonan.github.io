Professional Summary

Regis Konan Marcel Djaha is an AI Researcher specializing in the intersection of Applied Mathematics, Deep Probabilistic Modeling, and Scientific Machine Learning (SciML). He is currently pursuing an MSc in Computational Engineering and Intelligent Systems at the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU) (2026–2027), where he is directing his research toward the synergy between deep learning and numerical analysis in preparation for a PhD.

Prior to this, Regis worked as a full-time Research Technician at the Basque Center for Applied Mathematics (BCAM) within the Statistical Machine Learning Group. His research focused on deep latent variable models, particularly Variational Autoencoders (VAEs), and their applications to both unsupervised and supervised learning tasks. He also collaborated with researchers at the University of Oxford through the Mfano Africa program, investigating the visibility network structure of complex multivariate time series.

His academic background bridges rigorous foundations in mathematics, physical sciences, and modern artificial intelligence, with degrees from AIMS Senegal (AMMI), AIMS Rwanda and Université Félix Houphouët-Boigny (Côte d’Ivoire).

📚 Research

Regis’s research lies at the intersection of mathematical modeling, numerical analysis, and statistical machine learning. He develops robust, physics-aware, data-driven methods that combine solid theoretical foundations with modern deep learning, with particular attention to uncertainty quantification.

🧠 Core Research Pillars

  • Scientific Machine Learning (SciML): Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs), neural operators (FNO, DeepONet), surrogate modeling for partial differential equations (PDEs), and computational fluid dynamics (CFD).
  • Deep Generative & Probabilistic Models: Deep latent variable models, Variational Autoencoders (VAEs, $\beta$-VAEs), Bayesian deep learning, and representation learning.
  • Complex Dynamical Systems: Multivariate time-series analysis, visibility graphs, and complex network theory.

His current MSc work at UPV/EHU, including his Master’s Thesis (TFM), focuses on Scientific Machine Learning.
